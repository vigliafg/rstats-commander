## What it does

The **Hypothesis testing for the difference of proportions** dialog is the menu entry `Basic Statistics > Statistical Inference > Hypothesis Testing > Hypothesis testing for two samples > Hypothesis testing for difference of proportions...`.

## How to fill it in

1. Choose the active data set if the dialog offers a selector.
2. Select the variables and options you need.
3. Press **OK**.

## What it produces

The generated command is written to the **Script** tab and evaluated in
the current session. Printed results appear in the **Output** tab; plots
appear in the **Plots** tab.
